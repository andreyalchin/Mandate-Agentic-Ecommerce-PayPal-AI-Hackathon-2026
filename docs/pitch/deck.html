How it works

One mandate. Any agent. PayPal moves money only when the rules say yes.

1Set the rules

Pick a template or just say it. See exactly what the agent would buy before you sign.

2Agents shop

The built-in agent, or Claude Code, Cursor and any MCP agent, searches live products and picks the best.

3PayPal pays, or asks you

Inside the rules: paid from your vaulted PayPal wallet. Bigger: one tap. Outside: blocked.
